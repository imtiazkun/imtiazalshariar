The Gazelle Times is a Dhaka-based news agency I started to deliver fast, reliable stories from Bangladesh and beyond. A good focus would go into finance, politics and some niche communities.

Why spend time going through multiple news papers when we aggregate everything in one place saving time and effort on the readers part. Also have plans on releasing comics and other fun quirks physical newspapers have.
